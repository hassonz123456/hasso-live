Welcome to Hassō (発想): Master Your Financial Future

Hassō (発想): The Idea That Changes Everything

Hassō is the Japanese word for "Idea" or "Creative Concept."

We believe the best way to learn finance is through creative action, not lectures. Hassō is a life simulation game that turns confusing financial theory—like debt, taxes, and investment risk—into real, visual, and immediate consequences. Generate the 'Aha!' moment you need to manage your money with confidence.

10 Years of Life. 30 Minutes of Play.

Your journey starts now. Choose your university path or career track as a new graduate and watch your life accelerate.

Our unique, fast-paced simulation condenses a decade of life into just 30 minutes of real time. This forces you to master long-term strategy, where every single choice has compound effects on your future.

See Your Finances Come Alive:

  • Grow Wealth: Invest in stocks or savings and see the long-term impact of risk and time, not just daily fluctuations.
  • Build Credit: Your Credit Score is a live meter. Every payment or default instantly shows you the rules of creditworthiness.
  • Navigate Debt: Learn how loans are calculated and how repayment strategies affect your net worth.

Holistic Success Dashboard:

We measure more than money. Your success is tracked across these critical areas:

  • Money (Net Worth): Your overall financial health.
  • Health & Well-being: See how spending on fitness or reducing stress directly impacts your quality of life.
  • Credit Worthiness: The score that defines your financial access.

Hassō is the creative spark that transforms financial uncertainty into clear, actionable knowledge.
